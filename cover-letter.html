Pro Tips

The ideal cover letter is 250–400 words. Hiring managers spend an average of 7 seconds scanning a cover letter. Make every word count by focusing on your most relevant achievements and how they directly relate to the job requirements.

Generic cover letters are easily spotted and often rejected. Reference specific projects, team names, or company values mentioned in the job posting. Show that you've done your research and genuinely want this specific role at this specific company.

Instead of saying "I'm a great team player," describe a specific situation where your collaboration led to a measurable outcome. Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to structure your achievements with concrete numbers and metrics.
